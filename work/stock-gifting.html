Stock Gifting

It started as a wedding gift and became a FYERS feature: gifting stocks, ETFs and mutual funds in three steps.

In short
At a FYERS co-founder’s wedding, about 300 guests went home with gold and equity ETFs instead of the usual return gift. I designed that gift experience. It made national news, and I then took the idea to the team as a product: gifting from a FYERS account in three steps.
The result
A gifting flow for stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, REITs and InvITs, for birthdays, anniversaries and festivals. It works for people who trade every day and for people who have never owned a stock.

01Origin

A Return Gift That Made the News

The first version wasn’t a feature. It was a thank-you to wedding guests.

When a FYERS co-founder got married, the couple chose a different return gift: gold and equity ETFs, a small share in the market for each of about 300 guests. I designed the experience of receiving and claiming it.

02Challenge

A Gift, Not a Brokerage Form

The wedding guests showed the range: some trade every day, and some had never owned a stock.

The trader

Knows the market

Holds stocks and ETFs already. Wants to pick a symbol and a quantity quickly, without extra screens.

The first-timer

Has never owned a stock

Is sending, or receiving, a share for the first time. Terms like ISIN and quantity need context, not jargon.

Transferring shares involves identity checks, holdings and quantities. The task was to keep all of that, and still make it feel like giving a present.

Design principles

  1. Three steps, no moreFirst-time investors were dropping off in longer forms.
  2. Always show where you areA progress tracker sits above every step.
  3. Ask only what’s neededEspecially for someone who isn’t on FYERS yet.
  4. Warm at both endsA thank-you for the sender, and a message for the receiver.

03Three Steps

From Giftee to Sent

Who it’s for, what to give, and a last look before sending. A tracker at the top shows each step as it’s done.

FYERS · My Account

Select giftee: a PAN or phone number is enough to find them.Stock & qty: tick what to give from your holdings, and set a quantity no higher than you hold.Confirm & send: one last look, with room for a personal message.Sent: the flow ends with a thank-you, not a transaction ID.

Find the right person first

Before anything is sent, the giftee’s name appears with their phone and email partly masked. The sender can confirm it’s the right person without seeing private details.

Only what you hold, in plain columns

Step 2 lists the sender’s own holdings, so they can’t gift what they don’t have. Each row shows the available quantity next to the gift quantity, and the quantity field only opens once a row is ticked.

  1. Tracker: step 1 done, step 2 in progress
  2. Edit the giftee without starting over
  3. Equity & ETFs and Mutual Funds in tabs
  4. Tick a holding to include it
  5. Gift qty opens only for ticked rows

04Not on FYERS Yet

A Gift Can Be Someone’s First Share

Many people you’d gift to don’t have a FYERS account. The flow had to work for them too.

If the giftee isn’t on FYERS, the flow collects only the details it needs to deliver the gift, then rejoins the same path. Nobody has to open an account before they can be thought of.

05When It Goes Wrong

Mistakes Caught, Every Gift Tracked

A gift involves real money. Errors are stated plainly, and nothing is left wondering.

An error you can act on

A wrong PAN or phone number turns the field red, with a message right under it. The sender fixes it in place instead of finding out later.

Every gift has a status

Transfers aren’t instant, so the Status tab lists every gift sent. Each status in words comes with the one action that makes sense for it.

  1. Status in words and colour
  2. Success: repeat the gift
  3. Pending: cancel while you still can
  4. Failed: retry
  5. Several stocks in one gift fold under Show more

Cancelling can’t be undone, so it asks once more, in plain words.

06Receiving

The Moment It Arrives

Most of a gift’s value is in how it’s received. So the receiver gets a moment, not a notification.

After the moment, the gift is kept on record. The Received tab lists who sent what, with their message, and when.

  2. Their message, expanded with More

07Outcome

From One Wedding to a Feature

The wedding
About 300 guests received gold and equity ETFs as a return gift.
The press
The Times of India, Mint and ANI, through ThePrint, covered it.
The product
Gifting launched in 2022 for stocks, mutual funds, ETFs, REITs and InvITs.

A one-off gesture became a way for any FYERS user to give someone a share in the market.

08Reflect

What I Learned

Clarity and warmth aren’t opposites
The same flow that checks a PAN and an ISIN can end with “You are the best!” Precision is what lets the warm parts feel safe.
Design for the least experienced person
A gift is often someone’s first share. Designing for them made the flow quicker for traders too.
A good idea needs a story
The wedding gave the concept a real example and real press. That made it easier to bring engineering on board.

This is a retrospective review. Screens use sample data, and names, phone numbers and emails are masked or made up.

A financial product can feel human. This one started at a wedding.
