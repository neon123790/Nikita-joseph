    The insight
    Beginner traders aren’t short of information. They’re short of a safe place to practise. As one trader put it: “Varsity was easy to learn, but not to practise.”
    The outcome
    One app where beginners learn and practise on real past markets without risking real money. Two designers, neither an engineer, took it from Figma to a working Android app.
