Experience

From first launch to the Innovation Lab.

Three roles at FYERS, and where it all started.

FYERS · Bengaluru

Jul 2022 – Present Current

  1. Senior UX Designer, Innovation Lab · Apr 2025 – Present

    • Handpicked by the CXO to join the Innovation Lab, a small team that explores new product ideas with design, technology and AI. I own research and design, from the problem to a working product.
    • Designed and built FYERS Academy, a 60-lesson learning app for Android, in 3 months: every screen designed by me and built with Claude Code, Claude Design, VS Code and GitHub. One-handed, in light and dark mode.
    • Beta-tested with 15–20 colleagues before the public launch, and the feedback was positive across the board.
    • Created Learn by Doing: replay real past markets and practise trades with zero money at risk, so every lesson ends in action, not theory.
    • Led research and design for a new investment advice product, interviewing 50 investors on advice from people versus AI, trusting AI answers that show their proof, and how they research. Paused when business priorities changed.

  2. UX Designer II · Jan 2024 – Mar 2025

    • Led Portfolio, where traders see their trades and investments, on mobile and web, from first sketches to developer handoff.
    • Designed quick order windows on charts, so traders can buy and sell without leaving the chart they are watching.
    • Designed Option Chain and Advanced Screeners; both launched to live users and work one-handed.
    • Redesigned Home and Overview so users find their way and see their account at a glance, and helped rework the Watchlist with tags and pinning.
    • Designed a trading journal that collects every trade and marks it as profit or loss, part of the work that kept users coming back as FYERS reached 100,000 customers.
    • Interviewed candidates and helped hire 3 to 4 designers.

  3. UX Designer I · Jul 2022 – Dec 2023

    • Designed the Partner Dashboard directly with the CEO and CXO: one place for 150+ partners to track accounts, clients and earnings, and get support.
    • Led the My Account app over 6 to 8 months, from first sketches to clickable prototypes, including gifting stocks, accounts for children and address updates.
    • Designed account opening, onboarding and adding funds, all launched and easy to use one-handed, and brought NRIs and companies into one simple sign-up flow.
    • Designed a 30-day challenge that rewards coming back every day, and the News section, on its own and inside Portfolio.
