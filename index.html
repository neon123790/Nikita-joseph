Between canvas and interface

I’ve always been drawn to spaces and surfaces.

Paintings, patterns, architecture, and interfaces — I notice how things are put together, and how they make people feel.

Painting taught me to notice emotion, contrast, balance, and detail. Rajasthan architecture taught me that beauty can also be functional — that spaces can guide, comfort, and elevate the people moving through them.

UX gave both of these instincts structure.

Today, I design products that aim to feel clear, calm, and considered.

Let’s design something considered.

I’m open to conversations about senior product and UX roles, fintech, and products that need more clarity and calm.
