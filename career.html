Career

The road so far.

From engineering school to Senior UX Designer at FYERS — the roles, promotions, and products along the way. Scroll to take the drive.

  1. Start · 2017 – 2021

    Where the road began

    B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Christ (Deemed to be University), Bengaluru — CGPA 9.03. Final project: BEAM, a student collaboration app (UX and front-end, team of 5, 50+ students across 10+ colleges).

    NISM Series 7 & 8 Google UX Design Georgia Tech · Intro to UX IxDF · AI for Designers
  2. Jan – May 2022

    First design job

    UI/UX and Graphic Design Intern at DeFiner, designing logos, web screens, and mascots.

  3. Jul 2022

    Joined FYERS

    Started as UX Designer I, designing fintech experiences across trading and investing products.

  4. 2022 – 2023

    First shipped products

    Designed Onboarding, Open Account, Funds, Option Chain, and Advanced Screeners — all shipped to live users with one-handed use built in. Designed the Partner Dashboard with the CEO and CXO, the My Account app, and the News experience.

  5. Jan 2024 · Promotion 1

    UX Designer II

    Led Portfolio across mobile and web, designed Single and Multi Order Windows on charts, revamped the Watchlist with tags and pinning, and built a 30-day challenge streak and trading journal.

  6. Apr 2025 · Promotion 2

    Senior UX Designer

    Took on larger responsibilities across research, product thinking, flows, and cross-functional decisions — including primary and secondary research and end-to-end design for an advisory product.

  7. Handpicked

    CXO Innovation Lab

    Handpicked by the CXO to join the Innovation Lab as a UX researcher and designer, exploring AI-assisted product ideas and faster prototyping.

  8. 3 months

    Built a full Android app

    Designed and built FYERS Academy as a fully working Android app — front-end design from scratch, developed with AI-assisted workflows, with thumb-reachable navigation and light and dark mode throughout.

    Claude Code Claude Design VS Code GitHub ElevenLabs HeyGen
