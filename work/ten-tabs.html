01The overview

Beginner traders learn from everywhere and practise nowhere.

Videos, tips, blogs and unfinished courses leave them with plenty of knowledge but nothing that connects it.

So when real money is on the line, the plan disappears and emotion makes the trade.

We set out to build the one place where learning and practice finally meet.

02What you’re missing

The story, in six cliffhangers.

  1. 01

    We designed a feature everyone loved. Then users showed us what they actually needed.

  2. 02

    We gave them an hour to practise. They gave us six minutes. So we hid the clock.

  3. 03

    Then we found it: the one moment where every trader’s plan breaks. We built a score that catches it without asking a single question.

  4. 04

    We had 250 lessons to make and one founder. We needed just one recording.

  5. 05

    Everything was working, until we noticed something. We’d made the exact mistake we set out to fix.

  6. 06

    So two designers with no engineers stopped designing and started shipping. A new build went out every week.

One morning, we slid a phone across the boardroom table.

Leadership picked it up.
